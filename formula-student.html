More than a Competition

The Formula Student world has marked a definitive before and after in my life. It's not just about building a car; it's about pushing limits, teamwork under pressure, and engineering excellence.

2023

The Beginning

Joined the team in late 2023 during the UFS06 design phase. I entered the Aerodynamics department, where I worked with Solidworks and StarCCM+ to design and optimize the formula's aero package.

2024

Manufacturing & Victory

Later in the season, I worked hands-on manufacturing the package using composites like Carbon Fiber via Lamination. In summer '24, we faced reliability issues at Formula Student Spain (P14), but bounced back stronger to take 1st Place Overall at Formula Student Portugal.

2025

Optimization & Lead

For the next season, we improved our department's processes, optimizing the design and reducing the aero package's overall weight. In summer 2025, we achieved a solid P3 Overall at Formula Student Portugal and a competitive P7 Overall at Formula Student Spain.
