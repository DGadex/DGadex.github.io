My Story

About Me

Hi! I'm Edward Mena, a Videogame Design and Development graduate from Universitat Jaume I, specialized in Real-Time Visual Effects (VFX), Shader Development, and Technical Design.

I co-founded BackToBits Studio alongside university colleagues, where I serve as VFX Artist creating real-time particle simulations, custom shaders, and gameplay visuals for our upcoming commercial titles on Steam, including GoblinMancer and K-Boom!.

Through the Estudia i Investiga Program at Universitat Jaume I & Instituto de Nuevas Tecnologías, I've worked as an Emerging Technologies Researcher developing interactive Augmented Reality (AR) applications in Unity (C#) using Niantic Lightship ARDK, mobile runtime optimization, and responsive architectures.

In motorsport engineering, I contribute to UJIMotorsport (Formula Student), designing the aerodynamics package and developing the high-fidelity Assetto Corsa digital twin mod of our single-seater for driver training and telemetry validation.

In parallel, I conduct personal research in collaboration with external research groups, investigating Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs) and deep learning to augment physics simulation engines and explore new frontiers for video games.
